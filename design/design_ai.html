    A sidebar in the same clay

    Shown on wide screens (1024 px and up), where it replaces the bottom dock. Below that the dock is shown and the sidebar is hidden, so you never see both. The row for the section you are reading is highlighted as you scroll.

    Example

    Components

    Default rowHover rowCards

    Spec

    Card: --surface, --e-lg. Fixed flush to the left edge, directly under the header, full height. The toggle at the top cycles three states: button only, then an icon rail (76 px), then icons with labels (264 px, over the page), then back to the button. Rows are pills, 44 px tall, with a 32 px clay icon chip. The active row uses --primary-soft with --primary, and its chip turns --primary. From 1024 px up the dock is hidden, and it fades out over the footer. Below 1024 px it is hidden and the dock takes over.

    Sheets, toasts and menus

    Everything that opens on top of the page. On phones each one fits inside a 16 px gutter, so nothing is cut off at 360 px.

    Bottom sheet

    Slides up from the bottom. Drag the handle down, tap the dark area or press Esc to close. On desktop it centres at 520 px wide.

        Both primaries pass AA on canvas and surface: light #70A1FC on #EEF3FF is 5.51, and dark #9A7AFB on #1A1730 is 5.42. Two fixes in this version: light --muted was #667085 (3.19) and is now #6B6485 (4.97 on canvas, 4.56 on canvas-deep). In dark mode, text that sits on --primary-soft (badges, kickers, soft buttons, active chips) now uses --primary-active #B196FB (5.71) instead of --primary (4.34).

        Mobile rules

        Nothing may be wider than the screen. Test at 360, 390 and 412 px.
        RuleHow to follow it
        No sideways scrolldocument.documentElement.scrollWidth must equal clientWidth. If it does not, something is sticking out. Use the check below to find it.
        Off-screen startsAnything that begins outside the screen (slide-in animations, 3D rings, stacked cards, rotated tiles) must sit inside a parent with overflow-x: clip. The scroll-reveal cards and the card deck both broke the page before this rule.
        Popups fitWidth min(Npx, calc(100vw - 32px)). For anything wider than 250 px on a phone, switch to position: fixed with left: 16px; right: 16px. Never anchor a wide panel to the right edge of a small button.
        Gutters16 px minimum from the screen edge (20 px on page content). Cards inside a card drop to 18 px padding under 640 px.
        Fixed layersDock, toast and sheet respect env(safe-area-inset-bottom). Toasts sit 104 px above the bottom so the dock never covers them.

        Questions, answered

        Short, plain answers that people and search engines can both read.

        What is the difference between Quick and Deep?

        Quick builds 2 levels and is fast to scan. Deep builds 3 levels with more sub-branches and detail, which suits full-chapter revision.

        Which inputs can I use?

        You can start from a topic, pasted notes, a photo of typed or handwritten pages, a PDF chapter or a YouTube link.

        Is ThunderStudy free?

        Yes. ThunderStudy tools are free for students preparing for Indian competitive exams.

        Does dark mode save my choice?

        Yes. Your choice is stored in this browser and applied before the page paints, so there is no flash. Neither mode follows your phone or system setting.
